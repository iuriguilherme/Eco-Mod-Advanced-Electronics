World models

01

Drone Dock

3D model

The building the drones belong to. A player places one, assigns drones to it, and the drones leave from it, fly out over the terrain to work, and return to it to refuel and idle. It is the only permanent, visible piece of the whole system — the drones themselves are away most of the time, so the dock is what a visitor to the settlement actually sees.

It reads best as a landing pad or open hangar with obvious berths — somewhere a drone visibly sits. Roughly workshop-sized: walked up to, not entered. It sits in the electronics tier of the tech tree, so steel, concrete, copper and exposed conduit are all in register.

Right now it is a shape assembled from Unity primitives. It is the single worst-looking thing the mod puts in the world.

Scope One world-object model. Its inventory icon is generated by rendering the model, so no separate 2D deliverable. Largest single item on the list.
02

Drone role variants

3D · optional

Three drones do three different jobs. Survey flies a grid over unexplored terrain and reports what ore is under it. Mining goes back to a surveyed plot and extracts. Harvest works crops and plantations.

All three currently share one chassis and are told apart by body colour alone:

Colour-only differentiation was a stopgap. A player watching three drones leave the same dock cannot tell which is which from any distance. A sensor mast, a cutting head, a collection basket — anything that changes the silhouette per role would do more than the tint does.

Scope Optional. The smallest useful version is three attachments on the existing chassis rather than three new models. Listed because the gap is real, not because it blocks anything.
B

Item icons

03

Battery

Item icon

The fuel the drones burn. A player crafts batteries, loads them into the dock, and the dock hands them out to drones as they come back empty. They are consumed, so a player handles them constantly — this icon is seen more often than anything else on the list.

Sulfuric, in the mod's fiction: a heavy cell in a case, built at the electronics tier by hand. Nothing in the base game covers it, so there is no existing icon to sit beside and nothing to stay consistent with.

It currently ships as a flat green square.

Scope One item icon. Fully open — no established design to match.
04

Engineering Research Paper — Post Modern

Item icon

The research input for the mod's skill. It sits one rung above the Modern engineering paper, and the base game has no Post Modern paper in any family — so this is a new tier on the existing ladder rather than a variant of something that exists.

The constraint that matters is that the engineering emblem should stay untouched: a player needs to read engineering paper before they read the tier, exactly as the existing three do.

My proposal, entirely open to being overruled: keep the Modern star's silhouette but change the metal to platinum, and step the header band one shade darker. Rank by material, so the tier furniture never competes with the emblem.

#CAE4FF platinum #7C553F band

It currently ships as a flat white square.

Scope One item icon. Tightest brief on the list — the design decision is already made, and it needs drawing rather than deciding.
05

Advanced Electronics Upgrade module

Item icon

The specialty upgrade a player crafts to raise their efficiency in the profession — the same role the existing specialty upgrade modules play for their own skills.

It currently borrows the Electronics upgrade module's icon. That is a deliberate stopgap and it is wrong: the two are separate craftable items and a player holding both sees one picture twice.

Scope One item icon. Should read as a sibling of the existing upgrade modules, not as a departure.
C

Category and skill icons

06

Advanced Electronics skill

Skill emblem

The profession itself. It appears wherever a specialty is shown: the skill tree, the profession list, the star awarded when a player takes it. The mod's whole subject is autonomous machines that work land on their own, so it wants to read as a step past Electronics rather than as more of the same.

It currently borrows the Electronics emblem, which puts two different specialties behind one picture.

Scope One skill emblem, in the established emblem style. The skill book and scroll do not need art — those are shared across every profession in the game.
07

Electric Fuel

Tag icon

The category that says “this is what a drone runs on”. Today the battery is the only member, but the tag is what the dock actually filters its fuel slot on, so anything else electrical that ever powers a drone joins it rather than being special-cased.

Concept: stored charge as a supply, not a device — the electrical counterpart to the existing fuel category.

Scope One tag icon.
08

Post Modern Research

Tag icon

The category the new research tier belongs to, alongside the existing research tiers. Same relationship to item 04 as the existing research categories have to their own papers.

Whatever tier language item 04 lands on should carry over here, so the two are best done together or one straight after the other.

Scope One tag icon, dependent on 04's tier treatment.
09

Advanced Electronics Upgrade

Tag icon

The category for the upgrade module in item 05 — what the crafting tables filter on when they look for a specialty upgrade of this profession.

Scope One tag icon, dependent on 05.

E

Where to start

01, the dock, is the biggest visible change — it is the one thing on this list a player looks at every day. 04, the research paper, is the tightest brief and the quickest to close out. 03, the battery, is the most open if you would rather design than match.

Tell me which you want and I will send the reference material for that one and nothing else. Anything you send me I will conform to the game's format myself — flat art at whatever size suits you is fine.
